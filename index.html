Crisis management
International logistics
Time-critical problem solving

Getting a 14-person band to Israel when the flights disappeared.

During a major disruption, flights between Europe and Israel were being cancelled. A band of 14 people suddenly had no reliable way to reach their scheduled concert.

I took ownership of the problem and started working through the travellers individually. Some needed completely different routes. Others had already been stranded in different countries.

I found alternative flights, reorganised routes, coordinated people and kept changing the plan as new cancellations and problems appeared.

There was no single solution. I kept solving the next problem until almost the entire team made it to Israel and the concert went ahead.

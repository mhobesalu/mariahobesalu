04 — What I'm looking for

I don't just want
a job.

I want a reason to care.

I've spent much of my career solving problems, coordinating people and making complicated things work. The work itself doesn't have to be extraordinary if the outcome matters.

That could mean a company solving a difficult problem, a team building something useful or an organisation becoming better.

What matters is understanding why the work matters and working with people who genuinely care about achieving it.

Outcome over role

I don't need a clearly defined role.

I need a clearly defined outcome.

I'm most useful when the destination is clear but the route isn't.

Give me a problem, objective, team that needs direction or complicated situation. I don't need every step mapped out.

I need to understand what we're trying to achieve — then I'll figure out what needs to happen.

That might mean fixing an operational problem, building a team, owning a project, connecting departments or figuring out the next move.

Don't tell me exactly how to get there.

Tell me where we're going and why it matters.
And yes — that includes corporations

Structure and autonomy aren't opposites.

I can see myself working inside a large international organisation. Complexity is often exactly where I can be useful.

I like connecting teams, navigating stakeholders, finding gaps between departments and owning problems that don't fit neatly into one job description.

I don't need chaos. I just don't want to be limited by a job description when the situation requires something more.

The same applies to a startup or growing company. Size matters less than having room to take ownership and make something better.

Where I can make the biggest difference

When something is about to become bigger than it currently is.

I think I would be especially useful at a company that is taking off and needs someone to push the next big move.

The product works, the opportunity is real, momentum is building, but the next stage isn't completely figured out yet.

That's where I get excited.

Give me momentum, a difficult goal and moving pieces, and let me figure out how to push things to the next level.

I like being close to the action: figuring out what needs to happen, bringing people together, building structure and turning “we should” into “it's done.”

Give me a difficult problem.
Give me good people.
Give us something worth achieving.
I'll care about making it happen.
Another way to work together
It doesn't have to be a full-time job.

I'm also open to working on a project or service basis.

Through my own companies, I can provide services and invoice clients. If you have a difficult project, operational problem or transition that needs ownership, I can step in and help.

Sometimes you need a permanent person. Sometimes you need someone who can walk into the mess, understand the outcome and take ownership of getting there.

I'm happy to be that person.
